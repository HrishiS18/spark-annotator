Spark Annotator

Participants work with their own AI agent while a floating Flag button stays on top of it and the screen is recorded. Afterwards they rewatch each flagged minute and annotate it, and a codebook parser reads the transcript turn by turn to find candidate sparks.

Run a session with the Spark Recorder

  1. On the participant's computer (Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS) open /record in Chrome or Edge. Nothing to install.
  2. They enter their ID, press Share my screen & start recording and choose Entire screen. Video only, no sound.
  3. They press Pop out the Flag button: a small Flag button floats on top of every window, including their agent (Codex, Claude Code, ChatGPT…). Each press timestamps that moment; they can pick a spark type or just bookmark it to explain later.
  4. End session saves the recording (it uploads continuously while recording), they attach their agent's log, and the review opens, where they rewatch each flagged minute and annotate it.

Review & analyse

The dashboard lists every session. Open one to see the participant's flags, the codebook parser's candidates (with the rules and words that fired) and the before/after attribution on one timeline. Recording review plays the screen recording with flags and transcript in sync.
